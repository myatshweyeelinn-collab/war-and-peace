WAR AND PEACE

Understanding Conflict and Building a Better Future

War Peace Importance

What is War?

War is an armed conflict between countries or groups. It causes destruction, suffering, and the loss of innocent lives. War can also damage communities, economies, and the environment.

What is Peace?

Peace is a condition of freedom, harmony, and understanding. It allows people to live safely, respect one another, and solve disagreements without violence.

Why is Peace Important?

Peace helps countries develop, children receive education, and families live without fear. Everyone has a role in creating a peaceful world through kindness and respect.
